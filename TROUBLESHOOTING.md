# Troubleshooting IQ Voice

## The shortcut does nothing

Open IQ Voice from Start or the notification area. Check that it is listening and that the speech model has finished downloading. In **General**, check the shortcut and **Shortcut mode**. If the key combination is already used in another app, try a different shortcut.

## The microphone hears nothing

Search Windows Settings for **Microphone privacy settings** and allow desktop apps to use the microphone. Then open IQ Voice and choose your input under **General → Microphone**. Try speaking near the microphone and check for the recording indicator.

## The recording finishes without pasting

Click a text field where you want the output, then use **Ctrl+Alt+V** or **Paste last dictation** from the notification-area menu. If the recording worked, you can also find recent text in **History**.

## Setup asks for Vulkan

Update the graphics driver from your computer manufacturer or Intel, AMD or NVIDIA, then run the installer again.
